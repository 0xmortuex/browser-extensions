IMDb ratings

Ratings come from OMDb, which needs a free key: go to omdbapi.com/apikey.aspx, choose FREE (1,000 daily limit), and click the link in the email they send. The key stays in this browser and is only sent to www.omdbapi.com, together with the film titles. Ratings are remembered for 3 days, so a normal day uses a few dozen lookups.
